## How to grow from these

- **The question on the end card is the comment prompt.** Pin a comment answering it yourself.
- **Reply to comments with a video.** Every "what if X?" comment is your next video: `autoclip whatif make "<their question>"`.
- **Make Part 2s.** If one does well, plan a follow-up (e.g. "the zombie outbreak, day 100").
- **Tag AutoClip in your bio.** Something like "videos made with AutoClip (open source)" brings viewers to the repo.
- **Check the facts before posting.** Each one was checked when written (see below), but the `tooltip`/`stat` lines are worth a second look.

## Facts used (for checking)

- 01: Cornell 2015 US zombie-outbreak simulation (Alemi et al.): remote areas like the northern Rockies were safest.
- 02: Rabies is ~100% fatal once symptoms appear; wash the wound with soap and water ≥15 min (WHO); US post-exposure treatment = immune globulin + vaccine on days 0/3/7/14; incubation usually 1–3 months; ~59,000 deaths/yr (WHO).
- 03: 10,000+ Starlink satellites operational (Oct 2026); ~550 km altitude, ~27,000 km/h, ~95 min orbits; designed to fully burn up on re-entry; ozone/alumina effects are being studied.
- 04: Orionids = Halley's Comet debris, peak in October; most meteors are sand-grain sized and burn up ~100 km up; Earth gains tons of dust per day.
- 05: Saturn's main rings are mostly water ice, ~10 m thick in places; 2024 Monash study suggests Earth may have had a ring ~466 million years ago.
- 06: World oil use ~100 million barrels/day; G7 announced a 100M-barrel release (Oct 2026).
- 07: Death Valley 56.7 °C (1913); paper ignites ~233 °C (451 °F); lead 327 °C; Venus ~464 °C; aluminum 660 °C; basalt lava ~1,100–1,200 °C; iron 1,538 °C; tungsten 3,422 °C; Sun's surface ~5,500 °C.
- 08: 5.5B+ internet users (ITU); July 2024 CrowdStrike update crashed ~8.5M Windows machines; GPS works without the internet.
- 09: Carrington Event 1859: ~17.6 h Sun-to-Earth, auroras seen in the Caribbean; March 1989 Quebec blackout, 6M people, ~9 h; Solar Cycle 25 near its peak.
- 10: ~8.2B people; humanity packed together fits in roughly a small US state's area (xkcd "Everybody Jump"); Earth's mass is ~10^13× all humans combined, so it moves less than an atom's width.
- 11: 1854 John Snow / Broad Street pump cholera outbreak (Soho, London); 1858 Great Stink led to London's sewers; ~3.5B people lack safely managed sanitation (WHO/UNICEF JMP 2022); 2007 BMJ poll voted sanitation the greatest medical milestone since 1840.
